Hearts.

Coming soon. A browser implementation of Hearts with three AI opponents, written in plain JavaScript.

What's planned

Standard four-player Hearts. Pass three cards left, then right, then across, then no pass — repeating. Hearts can't lead until they've been broken. Queen of spades is 13 points; each heart is 1; shoot the moon flips the score. The game ends when someone crosses 100.

The harder bit: AI

The interesting half of the project isn't rendering cards — it's giving the three opponents a sensible play policy. The plan is to start with a hand-coded heuristic (avoid taking points, dump the queen on the leader, track which suits each player is void in) and see how far that gets before reaching for a search-based approach.

And then Star Realms

The longer-term goal is to extend the same engine to Star Realms, a deckbuilder with a richer action space. Hearts is the warmup for that — once card layout, turn management, and the AI scaffolding all work for one card game, the second one is mostly content.
