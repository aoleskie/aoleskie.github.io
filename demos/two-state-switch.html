A noisy two-state switch.

A population of independent cells, each in one of two states (off or on), each flipping back and forth at random. Move the rates; watch what happens.

fraction on: — predicted (kon/(kon+koff)): —

What you're looking at

Each cell is an independent two-state Markov chain. The deterministic theory predicts the population will settle at kon / (kon + koff). Watch the actual trajectory: it fluctuates around that prediction by an amount that depends on N.

Algorithm: tau-leaping with Δt = 0.05 s.
