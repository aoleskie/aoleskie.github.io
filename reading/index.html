A book per country

Reading at least one book by an author from every country in the world. The rule is loose: the author needs to have been born there or lived there long enough that the country is part of how they write. Translations are fine; I'll note them.

notes on the project

Why this, why slowly.

The project started as a way to break out of the well-worn rotation of American and British fiction I'd defaulted to. The first dozen countries are easy — there's a major world author from each, and most good bookstores stock them. The middle of the list is harder: smaller countries with smaller publishing industries and fewer translations into English.

I'm explicitly not in a hurry. A good year might be six countries. Some entries will be famous canonical works; others will be whatever a librarian or a reader from that country recommended when I asked. That mix is the point.

Suggestions are welcome — particularly for countries where the obvious choice isn't translated. Email is in the footer.
