A book per country

I'm reading at least one book by an author from every country in the world. The rule for what counts is loose: the author needs to have been born there or to have lived there long enough that the country is part of how they write. Translations count, and for a lot of these countries they're the only way the project happens at all.

    notes on the project

    Why this, why slowly.

    The project started as a way to see the world through its literature. Most of the list is fiction, a good deal of it science fiction, because I'm curious how different places imagine the future.

    I'm explicitly not in a hurry.

    Suggestions are welcome, especially for countries where the obvious choice isn't translated.
