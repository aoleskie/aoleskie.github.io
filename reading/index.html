A book per country

Reading at least one book by an author from every country in the world. The rule is loose: the author needs to have been born there or lived there long enough that the country is part of how they write.

    notes on the project

    Why this, why slowly.

    The project started as a way to explore the world via literature. Most of the work is scifi or fiction as I'm interested in how different cultures imagine the world and future.

    I'm explicitly not in a hurry.

    Suggestions are welcome — particularly for countries where the obvious choice isn't translated. Please send them my way if you have any!
